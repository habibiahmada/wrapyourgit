# Code of Conduct

WrapYourGit celebrates developers' years, whatever they looked like: a quiet year counts as much as a relentless one. We want the project itself to feel the same way.

This code of conduct is based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit other people's work and ideas.
- Respect privacy: never post someone else's GitHub data, tokens or private repository names.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Comparing people's recaps to shame them. A recap is not a ranking.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [habibiahmada.dev](https://habibiahmada.dev) or by direct message to [@habibiahmada](https://github.com/habibiahmada) on GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. Decisions are explained when that is safe to do.
